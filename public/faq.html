Acupuncture and Acupressure

What is acupuncture?

Acupuncture is one of the oldest parts of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Fine, sterile, single use needles are placed at specific points on the body and left in while you rest.

Does acupuncture hurt?

The needles are solid and about the width of a hair. Most people feel a mild, dull heaviness or a gentle warmth around the needle, then settle in. Speak up any time something feels uncomfortable and it will be adjusted.

Are the needles reused?

No. Every needle is sterile, used once, and then disposed of safely.

What is the difference between acupuncture and acupressure?

They use the same points. Acupuncture uses fine needles placed by a licensed acupuncturist. Acupressure uses steady, comfortable pressure from your fingertips, so you can do it yourself, almost anywhere.

Can I use acupressure during pregnancy?

Some points are traditionally avoided during pregnancy, such as Joining Valley (LI 4) on the hand. If you are pregnant, let Amber know so she can suggest alternative points.

Can I book an in person acupuncture visit?

The Rebuild Path is online right now. In person acupuncture visits will return when a new treatment space opens. In the meantime, online workshops and consultations teach acupressure you can use at home.

Does acupuncture replace my doctor?

No. Acupuncture and acupressure work alongside the care you already receive. Keep seeing your doctor and other providers.

Who is the acupuncturist?

Amber Smith, L.Ac., a licensed acupuncturist in Arizona and an NCBAHM Certified Diplomate in Acupuncture, with a Master of Acupuncture and years of experience as a paramedic.

The Rebuild Path Education

Consultations, workshops and CPR

What happens in a one on one consultation?

A 45 minute online session on how acupressure, meditation and movement can help you reconnect with your body. You learn points and practices you can use on your own. It is coaching and education only, with no hands on treatment.

How do I get started?

Book a free 15 minute discovery call. You talk through what you are working on, and together you decide whether a consultation, a workshop or the community is the right next step.

Where do consultations and workshops happen?

Online, by video call. Your link is sent to you before your session or workshop.

How do I join an online workshop?

Reserve your spot with the payment link on the workshop card. Your class link and joining instructions are emailed to you before the workshop.

What is a hybrid CPR class?

You complete the American Heart Association online course first, then attend an in person skills session with Amber. The online course is purchased directly from the American Heart Association.

Which CPR class do I need?

BLS Provider is for healthcare workers and students. Heartsaver courses are for workplaces, coaches, caregivers and the community. If your job requires a certification, ask your employer which course they accept.

Can you teach a class for our group?

Yes. Classes can come to your workplace, team or group. Call or text (928) 316 6511 to plan a date.

How long is a CPR certification good for?

American Heart Association course completion cards are valid for two years.

What is The Rebuild Path community?

An online community on Skool with guided workbooks, the Goals workshop in the classroom, and conversation and support between classes.
