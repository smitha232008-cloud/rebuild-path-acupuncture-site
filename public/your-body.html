When something feels off, the signs aren't always where we expect them.

Traditional Chinese Medicine looks at the whole person to understand what your body is trying to tell you. In this two part series, you'll learn its fundamentals in a simple, practical way, and how they connect your physical, mental and emotional well being.

What we will explore

Physical, mental and emotional

Physical

How your body shows you when something is out of balance, and what Traditional Chinese Medicine looks for.

Mental

How focus, sleep and a busy mind connect to what is happening in the body.

Emotional

How emotions and the body influence each other, and simple ways to support both.
