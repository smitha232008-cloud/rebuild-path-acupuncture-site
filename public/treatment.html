Treatment

What a treatment is

Fine, single use needles are placed at specific points and left in while you rest.

Each needle is solid and about the width of a hair. Most people feel a mild, dull heaviness or a gentle warmth around the needle, then settle in. Some people fall asleep, and others drift into deep relaxation. Both are part of the treatment.

The points follow a system developed and refined over more than two thousand years. Which points to use, how deep, and how many is the clinical judgment Amber trained for, and it changes from visit to visit as you do.
