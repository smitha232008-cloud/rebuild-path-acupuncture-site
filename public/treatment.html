Treatment

What a treatment is

Fine, single use needles are placed at specific points and left in while you rest.

Each needle is solid and about the width of a hair. Most people feel a mild, dull heaviness or a gentle warmth around the needle, then settle in. Some people fall asleep, and others drift into deep relaxation. Both are part of the treatment.

The points follow a system developed and refined over more than two thousand years. Which points to use, how deep, and how many is the clinical judgment Amber trained for, and it changes from visit to visit as you do.

How acupuncture can help

What acupuncture can help with

People come to acupuncture for a wide range of concerns. Here are some of the most common.

Pain and movement

  • Back pain
  • Neck and shoulder pain
  • Hip and knee pain
  • Foot and heel pain
  • Joint stiffness
  • Wrist and hand discomfort
  • Pain that travels down the leg
  • Muscle tension
  • Recovery after injury or surgery
  • Sports recovery

Head and face

  • Headaches
  • Jaw tension and clenching
  • Sinus congestion

Sleep, stress and mood

  • Trouble falling or staying asleep
  • Stress and overwhelm
  • Feeling anxious or on edge
  • Low mood
  • Feeling burned out
  • Low energy and fatigue

Digestion

  • Bloating and digestive discomfort
  • Nausea
  • Irregular digestion

Women's health

  • Menstrual cramps and discomfort
  • Irregular cycles
  • Hot flashes and night sweats
