Meet Amber

Founder of The Rebuild Path.

  • Licensed Acupuncturist, Arizona
  • NCBAHM Certified Diplomate in Acupuncture
  • Master of Acupuncture
  • Degree in molecular and cellular biology
  • Paramedic and CPR instructor

I'm Amber Smith, founder of The Rebuild Path and a licensed acupuncturist practicing Traditional Chinese Medicine.

Across all my work, one question keeps guiding me: what can help this person move forward from where they are right now?

That question has taken me from emergency care as a paramedic to teaching CPR, practicing acupuncture, and creating guided workbooks for personal reflection. My training in molecular and cellular biology and my years in emergency medicine shape the curiosity and attention I bring to everything I teach.

I enjoy making complicated things understandable. That starts with listening to what you're experiencing, how it affects your day, and what you'd like to be able to do or feel differently.

What I love most is building a relationship over time: learning what matters to you, noticing changes together, and adjusting along the way. My goal is to help you understand your body and feel confident taking an active role in your own well being.
